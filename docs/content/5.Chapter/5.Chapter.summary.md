## **Overview**
- Illustrates various document similarity measures and clustering techniques in R.

## **Main Sections**
- Euclidean and cosine similarity computation for document term frequencies  
- Hierarchical clustering with Ward’s method and distance metrics  
- Partitioning clustering via animated and optimal k-means  
- Model-based clustering using Gaussian mixture models  
- Density-based and affinity propagation clustering demonstrations  

## **Key Packages/Functions**
- tidyverse, lsa, factoextra, NbClust, animation, ClusterR, dbscan, apcluster  

## **Why this matters**
- Gives students hands-on experience with diverse clustering techniques for text and document data analysis in R.

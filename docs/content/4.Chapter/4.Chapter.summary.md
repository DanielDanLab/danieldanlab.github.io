## **Overview**
- Demonstrates text retrieval, preprocessing, corpus analysis, and visualization workflows in R.

## **Main Sections**
- Gutenberg text retrieval and word tokenization.
- Word frequency ranking and Zipf-plot visualization.
- Corpus creation and text normalization with tm package.
- Term-document matrix analysis and bar plotting.
- Bigram modeling, network community detection, and comparison.

## **Key Packages/Functions**
- tm, SnowballC, slam, RWeka, igraph, ggraph, wordcloud packages.

## **Why this matters**
- Teaches students essential text mining workflows from raw text to advanced visualization and comparative analysis.

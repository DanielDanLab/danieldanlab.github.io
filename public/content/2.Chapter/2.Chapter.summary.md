## **Overview**
- Introduces string handling, vectorization, CSV processing, and regular expressions in R.

## **Main Sections**
- Empty string and character vector creation and inspection.
- Combining, indexing, and concatenating strings with paste.
- String metrics: nchar, substr, abbreviate, and histogram visualization.
- Regular expression foundations: quantifiers, positions, operators, classes.
- Regex-based search and replace operations examples.

## **Key Packages/Functions**
- re2r package; paste, substr, nchar, grep, gsub, read.csv functions.

## **Why this matters**
- Enables students to master essential text processing and pattern matching in R.

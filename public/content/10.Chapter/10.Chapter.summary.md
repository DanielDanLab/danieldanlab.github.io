## **Overview**
- Shows word embedding techniques and transformer-based summarization and QA in R.

## **Main Sections**
- Training CBOW word2vec model and finding nearest words  
- Preprocessing and UMAP visualization of word embeddings  
- Performing vector arithmetic for word analogies  
- Summarizing text with T5 transformer model  
- Answering questions with RoBERTa QA transformer  

## **Key Packages/Functions**
- word2vec, umap, plotly, text (textSum, textQA) packages  

## **Why this matters**
- Helps students apply embeddings and transformers for modern NLP tasks in R.

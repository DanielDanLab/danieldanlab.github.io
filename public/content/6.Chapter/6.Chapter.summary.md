## **Overview**
- Implements end-to-end supervised text classification on product reviews.

## **Main Sections**
- Data preparation and document-term matrix creation  
- LDA and KNN classification model training and evaluation  
- SVM with linear, radial, and polynomial kernels comparison  
- Ensemble learning combining classifiers via caretEnsemble  
- Neural network and FastText supervised text classification  

## **Key Packages/Functions**
- Packages: tm, caret, caretEnsemble, kernlab, nnet, fastTextR  

## **Why this matters**
- Teaches end-to-end supervised text classification workflows and model evaluation in R.

## **Overview**
- Applies diverse sentiment analysis methods and visualizes results on product reviews.

## **Main Sections**
- Data loading and text cleaning  
- Multi-framework sentiment scoring timeline visualization  
- Sentiment word networks via bigrams  
- Emotion classification and rating correlation analysis  
- Topic-sentiment evolution and profanity detection  

## **Key Packages/Functions**
- Packages: sentimentr, sentiment.ai, SentimentAnalysis, vader, syuzhet, ggplot2, data.table  

## **Why this matters**
- Demonstrates practical sentiment analysis workflows and insights for student text mining skills.

## **Overview**
- Performs topic modeling and visualization using LDA and STM on text data.

## **Main Sections**
- Simple LDA fitting and topic-term extraction  
- Seeded LDA with custom dictionaries and sequential sentence-based LDA  
- Topic visualization with LDAvis  
- Structural topic modeling with metadata and effect estimation  
- Topic interaction analysis and optimal topic number selection  

## **Key Packages/Functions**
- Packages: quanteda, seededlda, LDAvis, stm, ldatuning; functions: textmodel_lda, textmodel_seededlda, estimateEffect  

## **Why this matters**
- Teaches students advanced probabilistic topic modeling and metadata-driven topic analysis workflows.

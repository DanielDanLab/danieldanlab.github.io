## **Overview**
- Performs NER extraction with spacyr and text summarization techniques in R.

## **Main Sections**
- NER parsing and entity extraction using spacyr  
- Entity frequency counting and visualization via ggplot2  
- Dependency parsing and linguistic complexity analysis  
- Sentence summarization with lexRankr and textrank packages  

## **Key Packages/Functions**
- spacyr, lexRankr, textrank, LSAfun, ggplot2  

## **Why this matters**
- Enables students to extract structured entities and concise summaries from text.
